                   Required Legal Interventions

Having come back to India in 2015, I am now becoming aware of malpractices happening across the spectrum in our indian lives. It seems to me that no government department addresses complaints in a transparent manner and follows it to closure, including the labour department (IT industry works 12 hours a day even though labour rules stipulate only 8 hours a day) and banks (RBI provides an online Ombudsman but complaints seldom get closure). Therefore I am starting to file court cases where I can. 

 

But I need lawyers who can work pro bono for me or atleast work for a percentage of the claims that I will be putting on these public interest cases.  If you as a lawyer are clever enough to make the exorbitant claim stick then you get a hefty percentage and also gain a name for running public interest cases.

If you came upon this page because of my advertisement, please go through the rest of the page on where I would like to file cases. If this interests you and pricks your conscience enough to make you strive to fix it.. then give me a call or drop me an email. You can also contact me on the contact page of this website.

 

1. Reform Labour Laws so everyone goes home in the evenings and spends time with family. I believe that once the mothers are at home to receive their children when they come back from school, the mothers will eventually teach our culture to their children and our Indianness will live.

 --- Right now the IT industry is leading the way for violating labour laws of 8 hours per day and rest of the industries are following. This can already be seen by late working hours in just about every office including your own law offices. All this for what?? So americans companies can still make the best IT products?? Do we realise there is no indian company doing projects for Indians.. Everyone is working on American or European projects. Meaning we work overnight so an american can goto a pub and have beer at 6pm on a friday. I have already started a case against Bosch in this regard with a stinking amount as claim. But we need a lot more legal fire power compared to what I am able to afford now. If you are a labour lawyer or law offices and would like to contribute to a coordinated attack to bring back the 8 hour work day like in singapore and australia. Please use the contact page on this website. Instead of wishing you were in singapore and australia or trying to send your children to singapore or australia, please do something change your own home.

 

2. Reform advertising standards. Nowadays everyone is advertising that their cleaning agent cleans 99.9% of the germs.. but do they really? Every detergent advt claims they clean every dirt possible but do they? We should realise that these brands sell superior quality cleaning liquids and detergents in Western Countries and sell inferior quality in third world countries like ours. That is why our shirts loose colour. This is not only true of detergents.. even electronic companies like those manufacturing printers, cell phones also follow the same principle.. they have what is called SKUs which curtail the functionality of the electronic devices which are exported to certain countries like India and others belonging to the third world. Whereas countries like Australia, USA get the best product. Have you ever compared a samsung phone which is sold in Australia and India? Have you compared the life time of an Australian/American phone and the life of an Indian phone of the same brand before it dies? And the advertisements for these devices blatantly sell inferior products to us buy showing unrealistic life situations which are hypothetical at best and end up selling it to impressionable younger generation.

Reform Media.. recently most of our media has come to be controlled by foreign ownership especially online and a few television groups also. We need to question and stop the spread of inappropriate content to just about every age group that these people can reach. The best example is the supposed webseries propagated and funded by organisations like Amazon Prime and Netflix. These webseries invariably inject atleast one sequence which requires age appropriate viewing. But these are fed to every person irrespective of age in India.

 

 

3. Nationalize management of online space and online resources atleast for the duration till the private industry is able to come up with alternatives for the monopoly of google and its subsidiaries, namely android. This will help our citizens privacy from being sold to google which invariably supplies this data to the spy agencies of its own government. Therefore, at a minimum, we need a national smartphone and PC operating system, email service, mapping software and a microprocessor. These have to be created and managed by the central government and should be made available to the indian public free of cost.

 Till the above happens, we need to legally question every single data that is exported to american servers from India. We do not even realise that google maps now correctly identifies even our most secret defense research establishments. We need Lawyers trained in Information Technology practices, cyber security and people who can use plain common sense to fight google which is in reality a national security threat although it provides som much information free of cost. People who realise this please use the contact page on this website.

 

4. Tax Evasion by foreign controlled enterprises.. For Example, google employs a lot of indians in india through its own arm or its subsidiaries. They are now declaring billions of dollars profits. It is important to analyse how much the indian wing has contributed to this profit and where exactly this profit is being made. Appropriate action in this domain can lead to substantial increase in tax revenue which can be re-invested in the national interests like the online space.

 

5. Pursue Iraq war crimes and crime of aggression by Americans by synchronising with wikileaks boss and file relevant legal action so Bush gets hanged in the next 10 years publicly.

 Although muslims follow the sharia and Sharia teaches them at a young age to kill every other civilization and build an islamic nation over the debris. We have to wait till the muslims themselves realise that their religion is endangering everyone and they themselves stop practising it. I am aware of the recent news that Darool Uloom Deoband has given a fatwa to teach young children ghazwa-e-hind or attack india. But we must realise that these children are indian semen and not islamic by genes. We must do everything to get back our Indian children to our fold without any threat to them. We have to sit down and collectively think about how to do this. But right now like in the hindu stories that talk about the sage who saved the scorpion from the flood by using a leaf and letting it scramble into a lonely place so it too can exist.. we must also endeavour to keep harmful teachings of certain religions at bay. But that does not mean not questioning blatant massacre of millions in Iraq. If we do not bring the perpetrators to justice, others will be emboldened to do the same to us tomorrow.
